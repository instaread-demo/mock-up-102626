Your Guide to the Dining Essentials of Quebec City

by Eater Staff
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by

Perhaps unsurprisingly, most of the maps on Eater Montreal focus on Montreal — but two and a half hours away, Quebec City has a robust food scene, and it’s getting better all the time — in both expected territory such as foresty Québécois fare and poutine, and some unexpected ways, with (for example) fantastic Italian and barbecue options.

Here are our guides that cover the city’s dining basics: the all-round essentials (the “best”, with an eye to variety), cheap eats, and brunch. When any new guides are published, they’ll be added here.

Eater occasionally covers food news from the provincial capital: to see the latest, check our Quebec City tag.
