COLLINS LANDING — State police divers were searching beneath the Thousand Islands Bridge Saturday morning for the body of a woman last seen on bridge security cameras shortly before midnight.

The search prompted an hours-long shutdown of both lanes while law enforcement, fire personnel and the Coast Guard searched the area, according to the Jefferson County Sheriff’s Office. The bridge has since reopened.
