Pamela Anderson Welcomes Future Daughter-In-Law After Son’s Engagement

By Sven Kramer September 22, 2026

Pamela Anderson has another reason to celebrate. Her eldest son, Brandon Thomas Lee, is engaged to his girlfriend, Katarina Demetriades, and the proud mom wasted little time welcoming her future daughter-in-law into the family.

The 30-year-old producer proposed to Demetriades during a romantic trip to Indonesia on August 29, 2026. The couple later announced the engagement through a joint Instagram post, sharing intimate photos from the getaway and giving fans a close look at Demetriades’ sparkling diamond ring.

View this post on Instagram A post shared by katarina ☽ (@katarina.deme)

Anderson, 59, quickly joined the celebration in the comments. The “Baywatch” star shared a warm message that made her feelings about the engagement perfectly clear. “I’m so happy for you, angels. You are a beautiful match. Now the adventure begins. May the force be with you. All my love, Mom,” Anderson wrote.

Pamela Anderson Celebrates Brandon Lee’s Engagement

One image showed the newly engaged couple kissing on a beach at dusk, while another gave followers a clear view of the model’s new engagement ring.

The ring features a large center diamond surrounded by smaller stones on a gold band. Demetriades proudly displayed the piece in the photos, while the simple caption revealed exactly when the proposal happened. “Meet my fiancé 8.29.2026,” the couple wrote.

Brandon added another romantic message in the comments. “To infinity and beyond, my love!” the producer wrote, making it clear that the pair were enjoying every second of their newly engaged status. Tommy Lee was equally enthusiastic about his son’s big news. “So happy for you kids!! Wowowoowowow!!!” the 63-year-old rocker commented.

The engagement marks a major new chapter for Brandon, who is Anderson and Lee’s older son. The former couple also shares a 28-year-old son, Dylan Jagger Lee. Anderson and Tommy Lee married in 1995 after a whirlwind romance and divorced in 1998. Their relationship attracted intense media attention, but both parents have remained important figures in their sons’ lives.

Who Are Brandon Thomas Lee and Katarina Demetriades?

Along with producing “Pamela, A Love Story,” Brandon executive produced “The Last Showgirl.” The film gave Anderson one of the most acclaimed dramatic roles of her career and helped introduce a different side of the actress to audiences who mainly knew her from television and pop culture.

Brandon has also moved into the beauty industry. He is a co-founder of Sonsie, the skincare brand associated with Anderson’s stripped-back approach to beauty. His work across entertainment and business has helped him build an identity beyond being the son of two major celebrities.

Demetriades has established a sizable audience of her own. The 22-year-old model and social media influencer has nearly 900,000 Instagram followers and has built a public profile through fashion, entertainment, and online content. She is also studying philosophy at New York University. Her background includes acting experience, with a guest appearance on the television comedy “Will & Grace.”

Before dating Brandon, Demetriades was in a relationship with musician The Kid Laroi. The former couple dated from 2020 until 2023 before going their separate ways. Brandon and Demetriades already knew each other before romance entered the picture. Both grew up in Los Angeles and spent several years as friends before their relationship became romantic in 2025.
